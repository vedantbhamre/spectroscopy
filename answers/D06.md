## Measurement concept

Test a liquid‑flow bypass cell with a short‑path (≈1 mm) NIR transmission probe (silica fiber) feeding a remote NIR spectrometer; in parallel, acquire MIR‑ATR spectra on the same samples using a lab‑scale ATR probe for comparison. [S01 p.5; S01 p.10]

- **Analyzer:** Portable NIR spectrometer (780–2500 nm) with chemometric software; separate FTIR for MIR‑ATR reference
- **Fibre link:** Low‑OH silica fiber (≤0.1 ppm OH) up to 30 m to the external analyzer
- **Probe:** NIR transmission probe (1–5 mm path, silica fiber)
- **Process point:** Bypass cell inserted in the process line, probe immersed in the liquid stream; MIR‑ATR probe used on extracted lab samples

## Spectral range

NIR absorption overtones (780–2500 nm) covering the O–H stretch region; MIR ATR absorption (4000–400 cm⁻¹) covering the strong water bending band. [S01 p.10; S01 p.9]
